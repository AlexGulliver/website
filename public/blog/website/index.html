Welcome to my second version of my personal website! I plan to update this post as I iterate.

The goal for this site is to have a corner of the internet where I can express, save and share parts of my life such as my projects, photography, writing and anything else that I am interested in. If you share any interests, connect with anything you see, have feedback or have your own site I would love to talk!

My old website was a lot of fun to make, I initially tried to create it with a pixel art theme and learnt to use Aseprite. However my tastes changed and I found maintaining and scaling the art to be a bit more trouble than it was worth.

This time around I wanted to make something simpler and more consistent theme wise.

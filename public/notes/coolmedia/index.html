Videogames

  • Dishonored (2012)
    Possibly my favourite video game of all time. Its ambience and story are perfectly mysterious and very immersive. The gameplay is really smooth and satisfying and gives you lots of scope to experiment. I think its a perfect game.
  • Fallout: New Vegas (2010)
    I love this game for its absurdity, setting and story. I've put a lot of time into this game and never finished it but I still love it.
  • Titanfall 2 (2016)
    I love movement shooters and I think this game is the most satisfying execution of the genre I have played.

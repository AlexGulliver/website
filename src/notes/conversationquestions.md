---
title: Backpocket Questions
---

I record questions that spark good conversations and they're great for meeting new people and deepening existing relationships.

Lighthearted / Breaking the ice
- Best sauce for chicken nuggets?
- When was the last time you mowed the lawn?
- Which of your senses is strongest?
- You’re a burglar but you can only steal things that slightly inconvenience people, what do you steal?

Getting to know people
- Do you have a fun fact about yourself?
- Are you working on anything exciting at the moment?
- What is your biggest goal right now?
- What book / movie / tv character is most like you?
- What is your favourite memory?
- What are the best conversations you have had?
- What is the perfect day for you?
- Explain your life story
- If you could wake up with a new quality or ability what would it be?
- Is there anything you’ve dreamed of doing for a long time? Why haven’t you done it?
- What is your greatest accomplishment?
- You can save one item from your house which is burning down? What is it? (Aside from family and pets)
- What’s going well for you?
- What’s a question you wish people asked you more often?
- What do you feel most grateful about in your life?
- If you could change anything about how you were raised what would it be?
- If a crystal ball could tell you the truth about yourself, your life the future or anything else what would you want to know?
- What do you value most in friendship?
- What is your worst memory?
- What does friendship mean to you?
- What roles do love and affection play in your life?
- If you were to die now, what would you most regret not having told someone? Why haven’t you told them?
- Are you happy?
- What’s your favourite thing about yourself?
